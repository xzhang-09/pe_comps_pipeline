8. Methodology Notes

Rank (Section 2) reflects overall comparability to the target, combining financial profile with business model, customer type, revenue scale, and end-market alignment — the table is ranked by fit, not by EV/EBITDA multiple.

The Comparable Fit Review (Section 3) is a directional qualitative review; it is not a substitute for transaction-team judgment, banker input, or confirmatory diligence.
